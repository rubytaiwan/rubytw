🏗️ Build N+1 Scanner with 30 Lines of Code 🏗️

With `ActiveSupport::Notifications`, you can build a simple N+1 query scanner in just 30 lines of code. There is no need to rely on external gems or tools. This talk will guide you through the process of creating your own N+1 scanner using built-in Rails features.
